The channel form has an *Open to anonymous visitors* control (on for a new channel) that saves
`anonymousAccess`, and the channel roster marks a channel closed to anonymous visitors with a
*Sign-in required* badge. The `organization.detail.after` zone contribution is withdrawn: an
Organization's channels are edited in the `organizations` module's own *Sales channels* panel.
